Our Story

Irobe Restaurant was founded in 2020 with a vision to bring the finest dining experience to Kigali. Born from a passion for culinary excellence and a deep appreciation for Rwanda's rich cultural heritage, our restaurant represents the perfect fusion of traditional flavors and contemporary gastronomy.


Our chef, with over 20 years of international experience, ensures every dish is crafted with passion, expertise, and the finest locally-sourced ingredients. We believe that great food has the power to bring people together, create memories, and celebrate life's most precious moments.
